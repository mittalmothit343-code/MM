ROKA PHOTOS
===========
Photos for Scene IX ("Our Roka"). Name them roka-1.jpg, roka-2.jpg, roka-3.jpg and so on:

    roka-1.jpg   roka-2.jpg   roka-3.jpg     <- Shown directly in Scene IX ("Our Roka")

Alternatively, pass an array of image URLs to `CONFIG.rokaPhotos` in script.js.


HARBOUR PHOTOS
==============
Photos from one particular meeting. The first photo of each harbour becomes
the flag flying on that island in the 3D scene; the rest fill its popup.

Name them {harbour}-{photo}:

    1-1.jpg   1-2.jpg   1-3.jpg     <- 30th May 2026,  The First Harbor
    2-1.jpg   2-2.jpg               <- 26th June 2026, The Harbor of Small Errands
    3-1.jpg                         <- 10th July 2026, The Same Harbor, Again
    4-1.jpg   4-2.jpg               <- 25th July 2026, The Harbor With No Reason

The harbour numbers follow the order of CONFIG.harbors in script.js. Add a
fifth harbour there and 5-1.jpg becomes its flag.

Rules:
  - Number photos from 1 with no gaps; the popup stops at the first gap.
  - Up to 6 photos per harbour.
  - The flag photo (the -1 one) is seen small and at an angle, so pick one
    that reads at a glance — a face, not a wide landscape.

Until real photos are here, the popups show generated placeholders and say so.
